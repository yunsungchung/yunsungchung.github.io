03 / About

Research across
data, models, and care.

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Tulane University (TRIAD Lab, advised by Prof. Jihun Hamm).

I study how machine learning can represent patient state and model how it changes over time, bringing together longitudinal health records, wearable biosignals, and medical imaging. My work spans forecasting, generative modeling, simulation, and the evaluation of decision-making methods.

Previously, I was a research intern at Samsung Research America (May–August 2025), working on wearable health sensing. I am open to Research Scientist, Applied Scientist, Research Engineer, and Medical AI roles.
